# Terrain lab textures: sources

The nine ground sets of the terrain lab's round 1 ([docs/terrain-lab.md](../../../../docs/terrain-lab.md)).
All CC0, fetched at 2K on 2026-10-18 by `unreal/Tools/terrain_lab_textures.py`,
which packs each into `TL_<Layer>_CH.png` (colour + height) and
`TL_<Layer>_NR.png` (normal X/Y in DirectX convention, roughness, occlusion).
