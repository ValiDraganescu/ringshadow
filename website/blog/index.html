The Ringshadow blog

How the game is being made, as it happens: what landed, what it looks like, and what's next. Every picture comes from the game, recorded by the agents that build it.

Follow it with the Atom feed.

  1. Build anywhere in the canyon

    15 October 2026 · M9 · 5 pictures

    No more build spots: place any building anywhere standable in a canyon, packed as tight as you like. Walkers route round what they can and bite through what walls them off; two cells funnel, one cell walls.

  2. The siege has a sound

    15 October 2026 · M9 · 4 pictures and 4 sounds

    Two music beds for the canyon waves and the Matriarch over the radio, 28 lines from KSTR's announcer for the canyon news and your next goal, and the Dropship's engines, all made with ElevenLabs.

  3. The balance pass: about ×2 the mining curve, never over ×2.5

    15 October 2026 · M9 · 1 chart

    Missions and canyon waves paid far too well. Measured against the pace model and cut: a pilot who does everything now runs ×1.86 to ×2.02 the mining curve, and level 10 takes 36 minutes.

  4. Two more canyons: the brine and the roost

    15 October 2026 · M9 · 8 pictures

    The Drowned Cut in Brine Pans, where the Brood's walkers wade at half speed, and The Roost in Dust Terraces, where every Gloamwing comes off a spire over the walls.

  5. By day you see the whole region

    15 October 2026 · M8 · 5 pictures

    No fog by day: every unit, bug and building in your region is sent and drawn, and dusk eases the sight down to the night's. Measured at the nest and at The Fork.

  6. Frontier pilots: you're no longer the only one out here

    15 October 2026 · M9 · 8 pictures

    Five pilots of the server's own in every world, with callsigns, paints and habits, mining, building, fighting and talking on the radio by the same rules as you.

  7. The realm under siege: the M8 showreel

    15 October 2026 · M8 · A video and 12 pictures

    I played to level 10 and asked "now what?". By the next morning the agents had built the answer and cut a reel of it: the Brood, the Citadel's guns, the Dropship, The Fork and the Matriarch, in 67 seconds.

  8. Holding The Fork: ten waves, and the Matriarch if you fail

    15 October 2026 · M8 · 10 pictures

    Tower defence inside the world: ten waves down the canyon's arms, guns on the rims, and if a wave breaks through, the Matriarch comes for every turret.

  9. The Dropship: no more flying alone

    15 October 2026 · M8 · 9 pictures

    Build Landing Pads and ride the Dropship between them: lift-off, two turns under the giant, a dash through cloud and a landing in another region, with no fade.

  10. The Fork: the first canyon to hold

    15 October 2026 · M8 · 8 pictures

    Two winding arms from Brood tunnels to a prize of 36,000 ore and 6,000 MH, and 12 spots on the rims for the guns. The waves come next.

  11. Valleys, not tiles: the regions reshaped

    15 October 2026 · M8 · 10 pictures

    Nine squares became nine basins: bays, rock spurs, islands, terraced walls, and one high land of rock running between them.

  12. A quieter HUD: the same moments, before and after

    15 October 2026 · M8 · 10 pictures

    One lane of toasts at the upper left, one banner slot under the compass, and a contract panel a third of its old height.

  13. “I reached level 10, now what?”

    15 October 2026 · M8 · 6 pictures

    The radio now keeps one goal in front of every pilot, from a chain of 15 picked by level and by what you've done, from pumping MH to the Matriarch.

  14. The Brood, painted: before and after the art pass

    15 October 2026 · M8 · 12 pictures

    Veined egg shells lit from within, opal chitin, glass wings, the Matriarch's glowing sac and crown, and the gatling gun painted from its concept. The same scenes, before and after.

  15. Through the walls: walking between regions

    15 October 2026 · M8 · 10 pictures

    Each region has its own ground, cliff walls and passages, and you walk from one into the next without anything moving or reloading.

  16. The world map: the real ground, seen from above

    15 October 2026 · M8 · 7 pictures

    M shows every region you've seen as a picture of its ground from above, with what's alive drawn over it and a tooltip on everything.

  17. The Matriarch wakes in Slag Pockets

    15 October 2026 · M8 · 6 pictures

    The Brood's queen at her nest: a slam, an acid spit and a roar that rallies her horde. Pilots who fell her share 3,000 ore, 1,500 MH and a rare blueprint.

  18. Guns for the realm: the Citadel shoots back

    15 October 2026 · M8 · 9 pictures

    Four turrets on every Citadel, a Sentinel AA tower and a gatling gun you build, and marks on each turret paid from your own wallet.

  19. Mercenaries: they mine while you sleep

    14 October 2026 · M8 · 4 pictures

    Hire prospectors at the Citadel. In a world day each mines what you mine in an hour, even with the game closed.

  20. The world runs on after you quit

    14 October 2026 · M8 · 4 pictures

    The local server keeps the world going on your Mac: the clock, starfalls, the Brood. The game reattaches when you're back.

  21. Coming next: guns for the realm, and a canyon to hold

    14 October 2026 · M8 Coming

    Four point-defence turrets on the Citadel, an AA tower, a gatling gun firing red-hot ore-and-MH rounds, and a canyon the pilots defend wave by wave.

  22. The Brood: from a painting to the world in a day

    14 October 2026 · M8 · 14 pictures

    The first enemies: four alien bugs that linger at deposits, bite, dive, eat ore and raid buildings.

  23. Missions, the Journal and three voices

    14 October 2026 · M8 · 5 pictures, 3 voices

    A board of missions at the Citadel, a Journal to keep them, and every briefing read aloud.

  24. The realm: shared buildings, a tax and a shop

    14 October 2026 · M8 · 6 pictures

    Every building now belongs to every pilot. The Citadel takes 10% of each unload and sells blueprints and supplies.

  25. How we got here

    14 October 2026 · M1 to M7 · showreel, 9 pictures

    Two days, seven milestones: our own server, a world that lasts, a hundred levels, the ringshadow sweep and starfall.
