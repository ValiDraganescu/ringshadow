# Realm projects: what the tax is for

> The realm wallet in Ringshadow now funds four realm-wide projects a world day long: the Beacon halves Dropship fares, the Relay widens every pilot's night sight, the Armoury lifts every turret a mark, the Dock Crew doubles the mercenaries.

Source: https://ringshadow.com/blog/realm-projects

15 October 2026 · M9 · A living frontier

The Citadel takes a tenth of every unload into a realm wallet, and until today that wallet could buy twelve marks for the Citadel's own guns and nothing else. Now any pilot can spend it on one of four projects that help every pilot in the realm for a world day. This closes milestone 9.

![The Citadel's REALM tab: the realm wallet at 1,000 ore and 300 MH, the donate buttons, and four project cards, Beacon running with 40 minutes left, Relay, Armoury and Dock Crew with START buttons and their prices](https://ringshadow.com/blog/media/realm-projects/cover.jpg)

*The REALM tab: the wallet, the tax, and the four projects. The Beacon is running, started by you.*

The [balance pass](https://ringshadow.com/blog/balance) noted the problem: one mining pilot's tax bought all twelve Citadel marks in six or seven hours, and after that the realm only filled. The manager's answer is a sink that pays everyone back.

## The four projects

- Beacon.: Dropship fares halved realm-wide, and a quarter when the realm has a Spacedock. Measured: a flight to Cold Fork cost 15 MH, and 8 with the Beacon lit. 2,000 ore and 100 MH.
- Relay.: Every pilot's night sight half again as wide; the headlamp is untouched. Measured at midnight: 673 cells seen, 1,179 with the Relay. 2,500 ore and 150 MH.
- Dock Crew.: The [mercenaries](https://ringshadow.com/blog/mercenaries) mine twice as fast, a twelfth of a pilot's rate in place of a twenty-fourth, their offline work included. 3,000 ore and 200 MH.
- Armoury.: Every turret in the realm, the Citadel's corner guns included, fights one mark up, capped at mark 3. 4,000 ore and 300 MH.

Each runs for a world day, 40 minutes, and costs about two to four hours of one mining pilot's tax. Every repeat costs a fifth more. Any human starts one at the Citadel's REALM tab, with the realm's money; the [frontier pilots](https://ringshadow.com/blog/frontier-pilots) pay into the wallet like everyone and never start a project. When one starts and when it ends, the whole realm gets a toast and KSTR's announcer reads a line: eight lines, 3.3 to 5.6 seconds, made offline like the rest.

Old saves load with no project running. Protocol 29, save schema 22.

## What comes after: the long game

Milestone 10 is planned, with no dates. The mode is called an MMORPG and has only ever been played by one human at a time, so the first task is two game clients on one server in a hidden run, seeing each other's Prospectors, buildings and flights and splitting a wave's pay. Then an away report when you come back, from the server's own bookkeeping, never invented; realm standings over a realm week, with a weekly reset on the radio; the Matriarch hunt, called by a human at the Citadel with the frontier defenders and the mercenaries gathering at her nest; a real settings menu; and a two-hour soak of the background server's idle path, the thing that runs while the game is closed.
