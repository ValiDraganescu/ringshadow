## Post

The realm in Ringshadow's online mode shoots back now. Every Citadel has four corner turrets, free, and pilots build two guns of their own: the Sentinel AA tower and a gatling gun firing red-hot rounds of ore fused with Metallic Hydrogen.

Ringshadow is an open-source Mac game where you are one pilot in a frontier world that lasts. AI agents build it; I direct.

The four corner turrets clear a raid of eight Shardlings and three Gloamwings in about 10 seconds. Their upgrades come out of the realm wallet, which the 10% tax fills.

## Thread

### 2

media: website/blog/media/turrets/04-gatling.webp

The gatling gun fires four shots a second for 16 damage a second, at the ground within 6 cells and at the air within 4.5, just past a Gloamwing's spit. A Shardling lasts 1.25 seconds in front of it. It opens at level 5 for 125 ore and 25 MH. The Sentinel opens at 10 and shoots only at the air, from 7 cells.

### 3

media: website/blog/media/turrets/06-bare.webp, website/blog/media/turrets/09-air-reach.webp

Every turret you place takes marks: +20% fire rate, +25% damage, +1 cell of range, three of each. On the left a bare gatling gun, the Gloamwing hovering out of its 4.5-cell air reach. On the right the same gun at 6 cells, hitting it. A fully upgraded gun costs 4,800 ore and 3,000 MH.

### 4

media: website/blog/media/turrets/08-chevrons.webp

The marks show on the turret: a plate of chevrons on its base, lit amber for fire rate, ember for damage, ice for range and sky blue for air reach. A turret that falls loses its marks, with no refund. That rule is there for the canyon, where the queen comes to flatten the guns.
