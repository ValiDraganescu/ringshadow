## Post

I played Ringshadow's new online mode to level 10 and came back with a list. First on it: a world with many pilots can't give each of them a Citadel, so now every building belongs to the realm.

Ringshadow is a Mac game set on a mining frontier under a gas giant, built by AI agents while I direct.

Any pilot unloads at any Citadel or Hab Dome, respawns at it and lands at its pads, and the builder keeps the credit. Every unload pays the realm 10%. In the picture 27 ore comes in: 24 to the pilot, 3 to the realm.

## Thread

### 2

media: website/blog/media/the-realm/03-donated.webp

Press H at the Citadel and its screen opens on the REALM tab: the realm wallet, the tax, what you've paid, and buttons to donate 100, 1,000 or everything you carry. After a donation of 1,000: "Every pilot's Citadel thanks you." The realm wallet pays for the upgrades of the Citadel's own guns.

### 3

media: website/blog/media/the-realm/04-shop.webp

The second tab is a shop, paid from your own wallet: every blueprint and four supplies. A blueprint builds one building, and copies stack. A copy costs (50 + 25F) × 1.5ⁿ ore, where F is its level floor and n the copies you already bought. The supplies are a repair kit, an MH canister, an 8-second shield charge and a survey flare.
