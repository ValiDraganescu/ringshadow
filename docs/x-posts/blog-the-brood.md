## Post

Until this evening nothing in Ringshadow's online world fought back. Then I asked the agents for something that would come for you, and they made the Brood: four alien bugs, from concept paintings to the world in a day.

Ringshadow is a Mac game on a mining frontier under a ringed gas giant. Claude Code writes the code and Grok paints the art; I direct.

My words to the agents: "Some alien bugs of some sort, they are always attracted to the POIs in the game. They come, linger around and live, if I am in their site they attack."

## Thread

### 2

media: website/blog/media/the-brood/01-shardling-concept.webp, website/blog/media/the-brood/02-gloamwing-concept.webp, website/blog/media/the-brood/03-lodeback-concept.webp, website/blog/media/the-brood/04-matriarch-concept.webp

An agent designed the family and painted three directions of each kind with Grok Imagine. I picked one each. Dark chitin the colour of the ore rock, opal flecks in the shell, light from inside, so at night they're moving lights before they're shapes. The Shardling swarms, the Gloamwing flies and spits acid, the Lodeback eats ore, and the Matriarch is the queen.

### 3

media: website/blog/media/the-brood/10-linger.webp

The numbers are built against the Prospector: 45 hp and a cutting torch. One Shardling is no threat, two cost a fresh pilot about half its hp, five kill it in about five seconds. You count the group before you walk in.

A Lodeback eats unguarded veins into its sac, up to 60 ore. Kill it and the ore goes back into the vein.

### 4

media: website/blog/media/the-brood/14-raid.webp

A director on the server spawns groups out of sight and sends them to deposits, wells, sites, pads, buildings and, most of all, fresh meteor craters. Raids go straight for a building, and KSTR warns every pilot nearby first. The start stays quiet: no bug lingers within 15 cells of the Citadel.
