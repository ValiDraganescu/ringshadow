## Post

“I reached level 10, now what?” I asked that after playing Ringshadow's online mode, where you are one pilot in a frontier world that lasts. The first ten minutes walk you through landing, mining and your first Citadel, and then the game went quiet.

Now KSTR, the only radio station, keeps one goal in front of you: the next useful thing for your level, from a chain of 15. At level 10 it's a skill on Q.

Ringshadow is open source, and AI agents build it while I direct.

## Thread

### 2

media: website/blog/media/now-what/02-journal.webp

KSTR shows the first goal you haven't done that your level allows. A goal you've already met when its turn comes is passed without a word, so a pilot who found its own way never gets told to do what it did. When the next goal needs a higher level, KSTR says so and names what that level opens.

### 3

media: website/blog/media/now-what/05-goal-done.webp

A goal sits beside the three daily contracts and blocks nothing. It pays about three minutes of play in XP at your level, plus 5 MH and your level: +240 XP and +10 MH at level 4. The chain starts at level 3 with pumping MH at a Derrick and ends at 35 with the Spacedock and the Matriarch.

### 4

media: website/blog/media/now-what/04-points.webp

The lines are KSTR's: "You're sitting on 2 skill points. KSTR can't spend them for you, and they don't earn interest."
