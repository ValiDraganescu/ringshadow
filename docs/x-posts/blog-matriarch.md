## Post

The bugs in Ringshadow's online world have a queen now. The Matriarch lives at her nest in Slag Pockets, two regions from the start: 6,000 hp, 9.5 cells tall, a slam, an acid spit and a roar that rallies her horde. She's the first thing in the world a pilot can't beat alone.

Ringshadow is a Mac game on a mining frontier under a ringed gas giant. Claude Code writes the code and Grok paints the art; I direct.

The Prospector's torch does 2 damage a strike to her, and after 20 seconds without a hit she mends 20 hp a second.

### 2

Her nest, from the painting to the plateau: a ring mound of chitin, dark rock and opal ore, 12 cells across, seven spines on the rim. Her crown glows violet, so you see her over the rim from 55 cells away. Four packs of Shardlings, 16 to 24 in all, and 4 to 8 Gloamwings keep it.

### 3

media: website/blog/media/matriarch/04-slam.webp

She roars when a pilot first comes within 25 cells, then every 30 seconds in a fight. The roar sends every bug of her nest within 40 cells after her target a third faster and hatches four more Shardlings. Her slam crashes down on everything within 3 cells in front of her: 40 to a unit, 60 to a building.

### 4

media: website/blog/media/matriarch/06-down.webp

What brings her down is turrets the realm builds at the nest, and several pilots at once. Everyone who hurt her shares 3,000 ore and 1,500 MH by damage done, and each gets a rare blueprint. Three world days later every pilot hears THE MATRIARCH STIRS, and the nest grows back.
