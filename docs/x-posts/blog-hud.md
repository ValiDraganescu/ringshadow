## Post

Ringshadow's online mode got a quieter HUD. Every feature this week brought its own message (goals, missions, contracts, raids, starfalls, the Matriarch), and they all landed in the middle of the screen, on top of my Prospector and each other.

Now toasts go in one lane at the upper left, three at most, and the big moments get one banner slot under the compass.

Ringshadow is an open-source Mac game built by AI agents while I direct: you are one pilot in a frontier world that lasts.
