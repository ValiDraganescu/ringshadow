## Post

Ringshadow is an open-source Mac game where you are one pilot in a frontier world that lasts, built by AI agents while I direct.

Press M and the world map now shows every region you've been to as a picture of its real ground, taken from straight above.

The game builds each region the way a client would and photographs it with a camera pointing down: terrain, cliffs, rocks, plants, ore and wells, lit at half past ten in the morning in clear weather. 8 pixels a cell. A script bakes the whole world in two hidden runs.

### 2

media: website/blog/media/world-map/03-prospect-landing.webp

Over the pictures the map draws what's happening now: you as a cyan arrow, other pilots, your mercenaries in orange, the Brood in red groups with their count, deposits in violet that shrink as they empty, pads as yellow rings, starfalls and their craters. Regions you haven't been to stay dark, marked UNCHARTED.

### 3

media: website/blog/media/world-map/05-tip-building.webp

Point at anything and a tooltip says what's worth knowing. A deposit shows how much ore is left and whether a Lodeback is eating it. A building shows who built it and its HP: the Citadel, built by you, 1,500 of 1,500. Click a mission or a site and the compass points at it.
