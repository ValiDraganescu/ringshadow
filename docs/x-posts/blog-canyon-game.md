## Post

The Fork now plays as tower defence inside Ringshadow's online world. The Brood comes down the canyon's two arms in ten waves, pilots hold the rims with turrets, and if a wave carries off 300 ore of the prize, the Matriarch comes out of its tunnel and fells every turret. Hold all ten and the canyon is the pilots' for a world day.

Ringshadow is an open-source Mac game where you are one pilot in a frontier world that lasts. AI agents build it while I direct.

## Thread

### 2

media: website/blog/media/canyon-game/02-panel.webp

The opening wave is four Shardlings every 3 minutes while a pilot is near, and it repeats until beaten. Losing it costs nothing. Then waves 2 to 10, 30 seconds apart. Gloamwings arrive at wave 4, Lodeback hoards are the mini-bosses at waves 5 and 10, and wave 10 is 34 bugs.

### 3

media: website/blog/media/canyon-game/07-fells.webp

If a wave gets through, the whole world hears THE FORK HAS FALLEN. The Matriarch comes out with six Shardlings, fells the turrets nearest first and goes home, and the canyon resets to wave 1 with the prize full. Kill her out there and the turrets she hasn't reached stand.

### 4

media: website/blog/media/canyon-game/09-held.webp

Beat wave 10 and the world hears THE FORK HELD: no waves for a world day, 40 minutes, and the prize is the pilots' to mine. Each wave beaten pays everyone who fought it, split by damage, turrets they built included. Wave 10 pays 4,000 XP, 3,000 ore and 800 MH to split, and a rare blueprint to each fighter.
