## Post

Ringshadow is an open-source Mac game built by AI agents while I direct, and its online world has a Brood of bugs that come for your ore. They went in as blockouts: the right shapes, flat colours. The art pass has landed.

Three materials do most of it, shared by the whole Brood: an egg shell veined and lit from within, dark opal chitin with a sheen of teal, violet and gold that moves with the view, and translucent glass for wings and sacs. The pictures in this thread are the same scenes, before and after.

### 4

media: website/blog/media/brood-art/11-gatling-before.webp, website/blog/media/brood-art/12-gatling-after.webp

The gatling gun went from a grey box to its forge concept: a tall furnace hopper of molten amalgam with glowing slits, over a ribbed gunmetal jacket. Its rounds are a white-hot core in an orange halo.

The cost: with 18 to 22 bugs in sight, the GPU's median frame went from 10.12 to 10.42 ms.
