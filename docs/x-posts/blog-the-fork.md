## Post

Ringshadow's online world has its first canyon: The Fork, in Cold Fork, cut for tower defence. The Brood comes out of tunnels at one end, the ore it wants lies at the other, and the rims between are where pilots put their guns.

Two arms wind down through a mesa from two Brood tunnels and meet at a basin: six deposits of 6,000 ore round an MH well. The richest deposit anywhere else in the region holds 3,600.

Ringshadow is an open-source Mac game built by AI agents while I direct.

Region files can now cut canyons and hold zones: tunnels, paths, the prize and the build spots. The region tool checks it all, every path on open floor, each tunnel at the foot of a wall. The red lines are the Brood's paths: 18 cells in 6 to 6.7 seconds, never more than 1.4 cells off the line.
