## Post

The world in Ringshadow's online mode now runs while the game is closed, so I asked for someone to work it: mercenary prospectors, hired at the Citadel, who dig your ore while you're away.

Ringshadow is a Mac game on a mining frontier under a gas giant, built by AI agents while I direct.

My one rule: in a whole world day, a mercenary mines what you mine in an hour. At level 5 that is 4,860 ore a day. It pays the realm its 10% like any unload, and it earns you no XP.

The first hire costs 500 ore and 50 MH, and each one after costs double. Wages are 5 + L/5 MH a day, paid at dawn, and a mercenary who isn't paid leaves. You get one at level 5, two at 15, three at 30.
