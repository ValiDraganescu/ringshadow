## Post

A pilot at level 10 in Ringshadow's online mode asks the obvious question: now what? The Citadel answers with a board of missions, and every briefing is read aloud by one of three narrators.

Ringshadow is an open-source Mac game where you are one pilot on a frontier world that lasts. AI agents build it; I direct.

Two missions in each of three tiers. Easy ones stay in your home region, hard ones cross into the next, perilous ones go to the far corners where the bugs nest. An easy one pays about 8 minutes of your level's XP, a hard one 20, a perilous one 40.

### 2

media: website/blog/media/missions/03-journal.webp

Press L for the Journal: each mission's briefing, what to do, your progress, the reward and where it is on the map. The marker shows on the compass and the minimap too. Five kinds: survey, haul, hold (stand in a ring while the ringshadow band passes over), salvage and hunt.

### 3

media: website/blog/media/missions/05-done.webp

16 narrations in three voices, picked by the mission's mood. The Citadel's mission officer gives the everyday jobs. The Warden gives the holds and hunts. The perilous ones come from KSTR's night dispatcher, on a bad line, scared: "Dispatch to pilot. Oh, no. They want a cull."

ElevenLabs recorded them once, ahead of time, and they ship as sound files, so the game carries no API key.
