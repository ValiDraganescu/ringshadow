## Post

On 14 October I wrote down what Ringshadow's online world needed once the bugs arrived: guns for the realm, and a canyon the pilots hold wave by wave. All of it has landed since.

Ringshadow is an open-source Mac game where you are one pilot in a frontier world that lasts, built by AI agents while I direct.

The gatling gun was the new piece: red-hot rounds of amalgam, ore fused with Metallic Hydrogen. Three concepts were painted. This is the one the worker picked, the forge, with a glowing furnace hopper on top.

The other two gatling concepts: the cradle, the gun between two arms over a glowing ammo drum, and the bunker, an octagonal base behind a sloped shield. The AA tower is the Sentinel, the strategy game's anti-air building brought into the world. It shoots from 7 cells, further than a Gloamwing can spit.

### 3

media: none

The part I was most curious about was the canyon. The bugs come in waves along set paths toward a field of rich ore, and the pilots hold the rims with these guns. The first wave replays until they beat it. If a wave carries off too much of the prize, the queen comes, fells every turret, and it starts over from wave one.
