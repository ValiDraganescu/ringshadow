# Ore cart concepts (2026-10-19, Grok Imagine, reference: art/models/prospector/painted.jpg)

## cart-towed*.jpg
Gritty industrial sci-fi game concept art, exterior. A small squat mining walker robot (round riveted deep blue bell hull, glowing cyan visor slit, a drill on one arm, a gripper on the other, headlamp on the crown, short heavy legs with hazard-striped boots) hauls a towed ore cart across red dusty ground: the cart is a heavy two-wheeled hopper trailer, riveted gunmetal steel with deep blue paint panels and yellow-and-black hazard stripes, big knobbly wheels, a tow bar hooked to a ring on the walker's back, the open-top hopper heaped with glowing violet stardust ore crystals and a pair of silver pressurised hydrogen tanks clamped on its side with hoses and gauges, an orange marker lamp on a short mast. Three-quarter side view, overcast daylight, rocks and ore deposit in the background. Different cart silhouettes across variations. No text, no lettering.

## cart-hauler*.jpg
Gritty industrial sci-fi game concept art, exterior. A small mining hauler vehicle the size of a pickup truck that a squat round-hulled mining walker robot (deep blue riveted bell hull, cyan visor slit) can drive: six big knobbly wheels, riveted gunmetal and deep blue body, yellow-and-black hazard stripes, an open-top ore hopper at the back heaped with glowing violet stardust ore crystals, two silver pressurised hydrogen tanks clamped along the flanks with hoses and gauges, an open driving perch at the front with a rail where the walker stands and holds two control levers, a headlamp bar, an orange beacon. The walker is standing in the driving perch, driving it across red dusty frontier ground past rocks. Three-quarter front view, overcast daylight. No text, no lettering.
