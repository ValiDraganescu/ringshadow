# Gloamwing concept prompts (round 1, 2026-10-14)

Each prompt goes to Grok with no reference image (the bugs are not the army's machines). The result is saved as `c1-<slug>.jpg` in this folder.

## Shared text

Paint concept art of a Gloamwing: a flying alien bug for a sci-fi real-time strategy game, about the size of a person, that hovers at rooftop height and hunts in flocks of three to six. It dives at its prey and spits a glowing bolt of acid from its mouth. Four long translucent wings. Design direction: {DIRECTION} It is native to Ashfall Reach, a dusty frontier world of dark rock veined with opal-shimmering Stardust Ore and pools of mirror-silver liquid metal, under a huge ringed gas giant. It is chitinous: a hard, matte carapace of big overlapping plates, charcoal with a violet-black sheen, crusted with flecks of opal ore dust. It is bioluminescent: a violet and magenta light glows from inside along the plate seams, in its eyes and through its soft parts. Painted as gritty sci-fi concept art with crisp dark outlines, worn, scratched chitin and grime. The game shows it from high above, so it reads clearly when small: a bold, chunky silhouette built from big, simple shapes. Show the whole creature twice side by side from a three-quarter front view, a little from above: on the left hovering at rest with its wings spread, on the right diving and spitting a glowing acid bolt. Plain light grey background, soft, even light, no text, no people. Answer with one image.

## Directions

1. **dragonfly**: a long, thin dragonfly body, a big head with two round faceted eyes, and a long segmented tail ending in a glowing stinger; dangling hooked legs.
2. **moth**: a thick, segmented moth body, broad dusty wings marked with glowing eye-spots, feathery antennae and a hooked proboscis.
3. **lantern**: a swollen, glassy abdomen that hangs below it like a lantern lit from inside, a small armoured thorax with buzzing wings, and long trailing barbed legs.
