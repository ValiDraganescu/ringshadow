# Dropship walk-in concepts (2026-10-19, Grok Imagine, references: art/models/zfight/dropship/after-3q.jpg, art/models/prospector/painted.jpg)

## shuttle-exterior*.jpg
Gritty industrial sci-fi game concept art, exterior view. A large heavy-lift transport shuttle resting on a square steel landing pad on a red dusty frontier world under an overcast sky with a huge ringed gas giant faint on the horizon. The shuttle is a boxy cargo lifter the size of a small house: grey riveted hull plates with deep blue team paint panels, two huge cylindrical engine nacelles on the sides with ring-shaped intakes, twin tail fins, orange marker lamps, yellow-and-black hazard stripes along the lower hull, a white cross medic marking on the flank. Its wide rear cargo ramp is lowered to the ground, the lit cargo bay visible inside. A small squat mining walker robot (round riveted deep blue bell hull, glowing cyan visor slit, a drill on one arm, a gripper on the other, a headlamp on the crown) walks up the ramp, only about a sixth of the shuttle's height, showing the scale. Three-quarter view from behind-left, ground level, moody rain-soaked lighting, wet pad reflections. No text, no lettering.

## shuttle-bay*.jpg
Gritty industrial sci-fi game concept art, interior view. Inside the cargo bay of a large transport shuttle, seen from the lowered rear ramp looking forward along the bay toward the cockpit. Riveted grey steel walls and deck with tie-down rails, deep blue painted ribs, yellow-and-black hazard stripes on the deck edges, warm orange cabin lamps and a few cyan status lights. Beside the ramp opening a stout steel post with a big round illuminated push button and a hazard-striped plate (the ramp control). At the far end the bay opens into a cockpit nook with a wide windshield, rain beading on the glass, two windshield wipers, and a console with a second big illuminated button (the take-off control). A small squat mining walker robot (round riveted deep blue bell hull, glowing cyan visor slit, drill arm, gripper arm) stands in the bay next to a parked two-wheeled ore hopper cart with a tow bar, strapped to the deck rails. Room to spare around them. Wide angle, eye-level of the walker, atmospheric, no text, no lettering.

## shuttle-window*.jpg
Gritty industrial sci-fi game concept art, first-person view from inside a transport shuttle's cockpit nook while flying. The foreground is the inside of the shuttle: riveted grey steel frame around a wide multi-pane windshield, deep blue painted ribs, a console below the glass with a big round illuminated take-off button, warm orange cabin lamps, two wipers mid-sweep clearing rain from the glass with streaks of water on the panes. Through the windshield: a red dusty frontier landscape far below with mesas and canyons, a square landing pad and a fortified citadel building with corner turrets in the distance, the sky filled by a huge ringed gas giant with its rings casting a dark shadow band across the land. Dusk light, moody, no text, no lettering.
