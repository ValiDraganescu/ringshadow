# Gatling gun concept prompts (round 1, 2026-10-14)

Three directions, one `mcp__orchestrator__grok_image` call each, with the
Sentinel's `concepts/base.jpg` attached (the Bastion, Hab Dome and Garrison as
the game renders them) so the gun stays in the same base. Each exact prompt is
in `prompt.<slug>.txt`; the result is `c1-<slug>.jpg`.

## Shared text

Paint concept art of a gatling gun turret: a squat defence building for a sci-fi real-time strategy game. Design direction: {DIRECTION}

It stands on a square 2 by 2 pad, the same small footprint as the Hab Dome in the attached image base.jpg, and it shoots at ground creatures and low flyers. On a heavy turret ring sits a short, wide rotary cannon: one cluster of six barrels round a central axle, the barrel tips glowing orange-white hot, heat shimmer above them. Its rounds are red-hot slugs of molten ore and metallic hydrogen amalgam: a short stream of glowing orange-white tracers leaves the muzzle. An ammunition drum feeds the gun from one side.

It belongs to the same base as the three buildings in base.jpg: silver-grey steel plates with panel lines, rivets and wear, a dark gunmetal plinth with yellow-and-black hazard stripes on its edge, deep blue team-colour trim, crisp dark outlines round every plate, painted in a gritty industrial sci-fi style.

The game shows it from high above, so it reads clearly when small: a bold, chunky silhouette built from big, simple shapes, the barrel cluster the first thing you see. It is lower and wider than a missile tower.

Show the whole turret from a three-quarter front view, from above, on a plain light grey background, in soft, even light. No text. Answer with one image.

## Directions

1. **bunker**: a low octagonal armoured base, the turret ring flush on its roof, the gun under a sloped shield plate with a slot for the barrels.
2. **cradle**: a round armoured drum base; the gun hangs in an open cradle between two thick side arms, a big round ammo drum glowing faintly orange on the left arm.
3. **forge**: a squat square base with cooling fins and a glowing orange heat vent at the back; the barrels sit in a ribbed cooling jacket, the amalgam hopper on top glows like a furnace.
