# Matriarch concept prompts (round 1, 2026-10-14)

Each prompt goes to Grok with no reference image (the bugs are not the army's machines). The result is saved as `c1-<slug>.jpg` in this folder.

## Shared text

Paint concept art of the Matriarch: the giant brood queen of an alien bug swarm for a sci-fi real-time strategy game. She is as tall as a three-storey building and many times the size of the small dog-sized bugs that swarm around her feet (draw three or four of them for scale). She never leaves her nest. A huge, swollen egg sac glows behind her; she roars, slams the ground with her front limbs and spits acid. Design direction: {DIRECTION} It is native to Ashfall Reach, a dusty frontier world of dark rock veined with opal-shimmering Stardust Ore and pools of mirror-silver liquid metal, under a huge ringed gas giant. It is chitinous: a hard, matte carapace of big overlapping plates, charcoal with a violet-black sheen, crusted with flecks of opal ore dust. It is bioluminescent: a deep violet light with teal veins glows from inside along the plate seams, in its eyes and through its soft parts. Painted as gritty sci-fi concept art with crisp dark outlines, worn, scratched chitin and grime. The game shows it from high above, so it reads clearly when small: a bold, chunky silhouette built from big, simple shapes. Show the whole creature twice side by side from a three-quarter front view, a little from above: on the left resting, on the right rearing up and roaring. Plain light grey background, soft, even light, no text, no people. Answer with one image.

## Directions

1. **throne**: rearing upright like a giant mantis queen on a mound of broken ore crystals, two huge scythe forelimbs, a crown of curved spines round her head, the egg sac trailing behind on the ground.
2. **siege**: low and wide like a giant armoured crab-spider, eight massive legs, a broad armoured head with tusks, the long glowing egg-laden abdomen swelling up behind her.
3. **crowned**: a towering, segmented, centipede-like body raised high, a great fan crown of glowing spines behind her head, clusters of glowing egg pods hanging from her flanks.
