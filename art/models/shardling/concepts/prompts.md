# Shardling concept prompts (round 1, 2026-10-14)

Each prompt goes to Grok with no reference image (the bugs are not the army's machines). The result is saved as `c1-<slug>.jpg` in this folder.

## Shared text

Paint concept art of a Shardling: an alien bug for a sci-fi real-time strategy game, the smallest of its brood, about the size of a large dog. Shardlings hunt in packs of five to twelve and swarm whatever comes near their site. Six legs, a low, fast body, and a head with two big mandibles it bites with. Design direction: {DIRECTION} It is native to Ashfall Reach, a dusty frontier world of dark rock veined with opal-shimmering Stardust Ore and pools of mirror-silver liquid metal, under a huge ringed gas giant. It is chitinous: a hard, matte carapace of big overlapping plates, charcoal with a violet-black sheen, crusted with flecks of opal ore dust. It is bioluminescent: a cold teal light glows from inside along the plate seams, in its eyes and through its soft parts. Painted as gritty sci-fi concept art with crisp dark outlines, worn, scratched chitin and grime. The game shows it from high above, so it reads clearly when small: a bold, chunky silhouette built from big, simple shapes. Show the whole creature twice side by side from a three-quarter front view, a little from above: on the left standing at rest, on the right lunging forward with its mandibles wide open. Plain light grey background, soft, even light, no text, no people. Answer with one image.

## Directions

1. **mantis**: two raised scythe forelegs held folded under its head like a praying mantis, four running legs behind, a narrow wasp waist and a short pointed abdomen.
2. **tick**: a round, heavily armoured tick-like body set low between six short, thick legs, a wide head with huge curved mandibles, a domed shell like a helmet.
3. **quill**: a long, low body like a centipede cut short, with a ridge of jagged opal crystal shards growing out of its spine like quills, glowing teal at their roots.
